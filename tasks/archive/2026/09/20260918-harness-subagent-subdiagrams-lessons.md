# Harness sub-agent subdiagrams — lessons

**Created**: 2026-09-18

Written while building, not after. Keep entries short and concrete — the point is
that the next person does not rediscover this.

## What surprised us

- The harness mixes agentic judgement and deterministic automation; showing both as “agents” would misrepresent enforcement and cost.
- A schema-level layout pass can still fail the final artifact readability gate when long context text is projected into the 1440px viewer; the frozen delivery check remains necessary.

## What we would do differently

- Start with one diagram per execution boundary rather than expanding the overview past 12 primary nodes.
- Keep relationship labels only where they add information not already present in node context or story notes; dense labels reduce trace readability.
