# Harness review lenses — lessons

**Created**: 2026-09-18

Written while building, not after. Keep entries short and concrete — the point is
that the next person does not rediscover this.

## What surprised us

- The three review lenses share one ordering rule but answer different questions: structural simplicity, defect/rule compliance, and harness/document drift.
- Repository-specific details such as the 80-point filter and the explicit `AGENTS.md` revise target are more useful in lens cards than in long relationship labels.

## What we would do differently

- Reserve primary nodes for judgement stages instead of representing all local automation as separate boxes.
- Align adjacent component heights before forcing a straight route; a one-pixel center offset passes topology layout but fails the final orthogonal-artifact check.

- None. These are diagram-authoring lessons, not product or repository conventions.
