# Perf harness measures v0.0.2 — lessons

**Created**: 2026-10-15

Written while building, not after. Keep entries short and concrete — the point is
that the next person does not rediscover this.

## What surprised us

- The harness carried its own workaround for Yorkie's defaults (`admin` as the password, Yorkie's guard entrypoint deleted). When the product started refusing the defaults (#201), nothing failed until the next repin. A harness that pins a release has to follow the product's startup rules as well as its image.
- The default browser channel fails at `chromium.launch` on WSL, which has no installed Chrome. A rehearsal there needs `PERF_CHANNEL=bundled`. The real rig installs Chrome (runbook §2.1).
