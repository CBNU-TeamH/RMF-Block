## Congestion presets (#193)

**Method**
- **Where**: G only, with `tc netem` and an `ifb` ingress on G's Wi-Fi interface. The filter matches only H's ports 3000 and 8080, so the harness's control channel to G is not slowed.
- **Amount**: each direction gets half the preset's added RTT and half its loss, with no rate limit. H's own clients reach the server over loopback and stay at baseline.
- **Switch**: a small HTTP switch on G applied the preset and removed it. Its `status` output is logged before and after every cell.
- **Removal check**: the stack-on reference `ref-on` ran right after the last preset and measured a G RTT median of 8 ms.
- **moderate is not measured.** The school Wi-Fi measurement (#193 step 1) was not done, so moderate uses the criteria doc's provisional values.

| Preset | Added RTT / loss (round trip) | Per direction | Harness HTTP probe, G RTT median (reported run) |
|---|---|---|---|
| baseline (`ref-on`) | — | — | 8 ms |
| light | +40 ms / 1 % | +20 ms / 0.5 % | 90–109 ms |
| moderate (provisional, not measured) | +120 ms / 3 % | +60 ms / 1.5 % | 252–253 ms |
| heavy | +300 ms / 10 % | +150 ms / 5 % | 612–613 ms |

## Outage recovery (#193)

**Protocol**
- The round runs a fresh stack with 8 users and 2 minutes of edits and chat.
- The harness then cuts G's Yorkie streams and app sockets for 20 s or 40 s. Both sides keep editing during the cut. G also sends one chat, which fails.
- After the restore, the harness presses resend, runs 2 more minutes of work, and checks convergence.
- Times are measured from the restore.
- These rounds' p95 (002 ≤ 207 ms, 004 ≤ 51 ms, on the non-outage markers) is not comparable to the baseline cells.

| Cell | Rounds | Document resync | Chat backfill (case ①) | Chat socket reopen | Session notification | Failed send shown (②) | Resend delivered (③) | Lost / duplicated |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20 s (`outage20`) | 5 | 365–473 ms | 9.9–10.0 s | 9.9–10.0 s | never reopened | 5/5 | 69–129 ms | 0 / 0 |
| 40 s (`outage40`) | 3 | 657–668 ms | 4.9–5.0 s | 4.9–5.0 s | never reopened | 3/3 | 54–112 ms | 0 / 0 |

- **Document**: all edits made on either side during the cut arrived after the restore, and every edit marker appears exactly once in the converged document.
- **Chat**
  - ① H's chats sent during the cut reached G once the socket reopened. They arrived exactly once on both H and G.
  - ② G's send during the cut showed "전송 실패 · 다시 보내기".
  - ③ The explicit resend was delivered once.
- **Backfill waits on the reconnect backoff.** The chat socket reconnects on an exponential backoff (1, 2, 4, 8 … s, capped at 15 s), so after a 20 s cut the next attempt falls about 10 s after the restore, and after a 40 s cut about 5 s (#241).
- **The session notification socket never reopened** in any round (#37). It is reported separately and was not worked around.
- **Unmatched / dup column.** Each `summary.md` shows 3–10 in that column for 002 and 004. These are the outage-phase markers, which are excluded from the latency pool by design. They are not duplicates.
- **Server and client memory**: client peaks were H ≤ 126 MiB and G ≤ 92 MiB. Server peaks were app ≤ 48 % / 155 MiB, MongoDB ≤ 61 % / 347 MiB and Yorkie ≤ 14 % / 104 MiB.

## Port blackhole (#193, supplementary)

**What was done.** One round (`blackhole`, 8 users, 2-minute window, stack on). 95 s into the round, G dropped 100 % of traffic to H's ports 3000 and 8080 for 20 s.

**It was a stall, not a reconnect.** No socket closed, no Watch stream ended, and the round logged no failure signal. TCP held the connections through the drop.

**What arrived late.** Edits made during the blackhole were flushed about 9 s after it lifted, because TCP's retransmission backoff had grown during the drop. That pushed 3 of 20 edits each way and 1 of 10 chats each way past the harness's 10 s attempt timeout, so `summary.md` shows 002 p95 ∞. After the blackhole lifted, chats arrived in about 2.5 s H→G and about 11 s G→H.

**Nothing was lost or duplicated.** Convergence and the exactly-once checks passed. The presenter phase after the blackhole measured 202 / 204 ms.
