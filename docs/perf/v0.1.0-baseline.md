## Congestion presets (#193)

**Method.** The presets and how they are applied are in the criteria doc, under "시나리오 프리셋" and "degradation을 거는 방법". As run:
- **Applied with:** `tc netem` and an `ifb` ingress on G's Wi-Fi, filtered to H's ports 3000 and 8080. A small HTTP switch on G applied each preset and logged its `status` before and after every cell.
- **Removal check:** `ref-on` ran right after the last preset and measured a G RTT median of 8 ms.
- **moderate is provisional:** the school Wi-Fi was not measured (#193 step 1).

| Preset | Added RTT / loss | Harness HTTP probe, G RTT median |
|---|---|---|
| baseline (`ref-on`) | — | 8 ms |
| light | +40 ms / 1 % | 90–109 ms |
| moderate (provisional) | +120 ms / 3 % | 252–253 ms |
| heavy | +300 ms / 10 % | 612–613 ms |

The probe opens a new connection per sample, so one sample covers about two round trips. Its rise over baseline, about 2 × the added RTT, shows the preset was in place.

## Outage recovery (#193)

**Protocol.** The criteria doc's "재연결" protocol. The harness cuts G's Yorkie streams and app sockets for 20 s or 40 s. Times are counted from the restore.

| Cell | Rounds | Document resync | ① H's chats backfilled to G | Chat socket reopen | Session notification | ② failed send shown | ③ resend delivered | Lost / duplicated |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20 s (`outage20`) | 5 | 365–473 ms | 9.9–10.0 s | 9.9–10.0 s | never reopened | 5/5 | 69–129 ms | 0 / 0 |
| 40 s (`outage40`) | 3 | 657–668 ms | 4.9–5.0 s | 4.9–5.0 s | never reopened | 3/3 | 54–112 ms | 0 / 0 |

- **Chat:** the socket reconnects on the backoff in [the chat design](../design/chat.md). Its next attempt lands about 10 s after a 20 s cut is restored, and about 5 s after a 40 s cut (#241).
- **Session notification:** reported separately (#37) and not worked around.
- **`summary.md`'s "Unmatched / dup" column (3–10 here):** these are the outage-phase markers, which the latency pool leaves out. They are not duplicates. "Lost / duplicated" above comes from the convergence and exactly-once checks.

## Port blackhole (#193, supplementary)

The `blackhole` cell is one round: 8 users, a 2-minute window. 95 s in, G dropped all traffic to H's ports 3000 and 8080 for 20 s.

- **It stalled; it did not reconnect.** No socket closed, and the round logged no failure signal.
- **Edits arrived late.** Edits made during the drop were flushed about 9 s after it lifted, slowed by TCP's retransmission backoff.
  - That put 3 of 20 edits and 1 of 10 chats each way past the 10 s attempt timeout, so 002's p95 shows ∞.
  - Chats arrived about 2.5 s (H→G) and 11 s (G→H) after the lift.
- **Nothing was lost or duplicated.** The presenter phase after the drop measured 202 / 204 ms.
